Add a sensor

  1. Buy a Z-Wave temperature and humidity sensor (US 908 MHz), or a water leak sensor. Zooz ZSE44 (temperature and humidity) and Zooz water leak sensors both work.
  2. Open Z-Wave JS UI (link in Settings), choose Manage Nodes → Inclusion → Default, and wake the sensor as its manual says. Accept the S2 security options and enter the PIN from its label if asked.
  3. In Z-Wave JS UI, click the node, type a name and press the arrow next to the Name field. It appears here within a minute, room sensors under Room sensors and leak sensors under Water leak sensors.

      Connection

      Z-Wave setup checklist
      1. Plug the Z-Wave USB stick in with an extension cable, a few feet from the Umbrel. Use a USB-C adapter if the stick is USB-A.
      2. Open Z-Wave JS UI on port 8091 of your Umbrel (http://umbrel.local:8091).
      3. Settings → Z-Wave: pick the stick as the serial port, click the circular arrows next to each empty security key to generate it, set RF region to USA, then Save.
      4. Settings → Home Assistant: turn on WS Server (port 3000). Keep Home Assistant mode off.
      5. Wake the T6 Pro and include it: the round blue button at the bottom right → Manage Nodes → Inclusion → Default. Enter the PIN from its label if asked, then wait for the interview to finish.
      6. Come back here, switch to Live.

      Notifications (ntfy)

      Sends push alerts to your phone through ntfy. Install the ntfy app, subscribe to a topic name only you know, and enter it here. Every alert below is off until you turn it on. Anyone who knows an ntfy.sh topic can read it, so pick a long random name or use your own ntfy server.

      Control

      Room-aware raises the heat setpoint when that room is colder than the schedule target, and lowers the cool setpoint when it is warmer. It re-checks every 10 minutes and never goes past your safety limits.

      Safety limits

      Nothing Cold Front sends can go outside these, whatever the schedule or a hold says.
