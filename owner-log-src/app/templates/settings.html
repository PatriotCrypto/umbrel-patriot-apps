Import flights

A CSV with date and hours columns. An export from Pilot Logbook, ForeFlight or a spreadsheet works — column names are matched loosely, and an unknown pilot name is added to the people list. Flights are added, never replaced, and the airframe and engine times go up by the imported hours.

A word on what this is

Owner Log keeps your records. It does not replace the aircraft's permanent logbooks, and nothing it prints is a maintenance release or a return to service. Due dates are worked out from the intervals you enter — check them against your airworthiness limitations, the manufacturer's schedule and the regulations that apply to you.
